Match Settings

In-Game Configuration

Mode: Bomb (Standard)
Rounds per Map: First to 4 wins (max 7; OT at 3-3)
Round Timer: 3 minutes
Attacker Rush: Off
Damage Indicator: On
Friendly Fire: On
Reverse Friendly Fire: On
Operator Bans: 2 per team per map, alternating

Map & Operator Bans

Series Procedure

1
Map Selection: Each team bans 3 maps from the pool. Coin flip determines ban order. Remaining 2 maps are played in the series; a third game uses a mutual pick or random selection if needed.
2
Operator Bans: Before each map, both teams have 30 seconds to ban 2 operators each (total 4 bans per map). Bans alternate: Team A bans, Team B bans, Team A bans, Team B bans.
3
Side Selection: Coin flip winner picks their starting side (attack or defense) for each map. Teams switch sides at halftime (after 6 rounds).
4
Overtime: If a map reaches 3-3, one overtime round is played. Overtime uses a coin flip for side selection. If still tied after OT, a second OT round is played with sides swapped.
Conduct

Player Conduct & Integrity

  • ▶ All matches are played in custom lobbies (Siege's custom game feature) with both teams in the lobby.
  • ▶ Results must be submitted with a screenshot of the final round score per map within 30 minutes of match completion.
  • ▶ Pausing is permitted for technical issues only. Notify the opposing team via in-game text chat before pausing.
  • ▶ Unsportsmanlike behavior, slurs, or harassment in voice/text chat are grounds for suspension.
  • ▶ All players must have an active Ubisoft account verified by NCEO. Accounts must not be under an active Ubisoft ban.
