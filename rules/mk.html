Rulebook

Mario Kart

1v1 Head-to-Head & 6v6 Team Racing — Official NCEO competition rules.

1v1 + 6v6
Format
Nintendo Switch
Platform
Up to 6
Players / School
150cc
Speed Class
Match Settings

In-Game Configuration

1v1 Settings

  • Speed: 150cc
  • Items: Normal (all items enabled)
  • Laps: 3
  • Races per Match: Best of 3 courses
  • Computer Players: None
  • Teams: Off

6v6 Team Settings

  • Speed: 150cc
  • Items: Normal
  • Laps: 3
  • Races per Match: 4 races
  • Teams: On (3 players per team color)
  • Scoring: Cumulative points across all 4 races
Course Selection

1v1 Course Selection Process

1
Game 1: Each player bans 1 course from the standard 12-course pool. Players alternate picking from remaining courses. Loser of the coin flip picks first ban; winner picks first course.
2
Games 2 & 3: The losing player of the previous race picks the next course. The winning player may not counter-pick a course they already won on.
3
6v6: Both schools agree on 4 courses before the match. If no agreement, courses are randomly selected from the standard pool. No repeats within a single match.
Scoring

Points & Tiebreakers

  • ▶ 1v1: First player to win 2 of 3 races wins the match.
  • ▶ 6v6: Standard Mario Kart VS mode points apply. The team with the highest cumulative point total after 4 races wins.
  • ▶ Tiebreaker (6v6): If tied after 4 races, one additional tiebreaker race is played on a mutually agreed course.
  • ▶ All players must use their own Nintendo Switch Online connection. No sharing of accounts or consoles during an active match.
Conduct

Player Conduct & Integrity

  • ▶ All players must be enrolled at their registered school with a valid .edu email.
  • ▶ Match results and a screenshot of the final standings screen must be submitted to the portal within 30 minutes.
  • ▶ Intentional griefing (purposely letting opponents pass, driving in reverse) is prohibited and will result in a forfeit.
  • ▶ Technical disconnects before the first lap is completed result in a replay. Disconnects after are recorded as the final standing at time of disconnect.
