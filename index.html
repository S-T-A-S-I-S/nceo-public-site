Supported Titles

Compete Across 6 Games

Each title runs its own conference schedule with dedicated brackets, standings, and season championships.

Super Smash Bros

SSB · Fighting

1v1 bracket format with double elimination. Compete in one of collegiate esports' most popular fighting games.

1v1 · Double Elim Season 3

Mario Kart

MK · Racing

Head-to-head races with custom point systems. One of the most accessible titles for new competitors.

1v1 · Points Season 3

Rocket League

RL · Sports

Team-based vehicular soccer in a best-of-3 match format. One of the most watched collegiate esports titles.

3v3 · Bo3 Season 3

Fortnite

FN · Battle Royale

Solo and duo competitive formats. Build, fight, and outlast opponents in structured tournament play.

1v1 · Duos Season 3

Rainbow Six Siege

R6 · Tactical FPS

5v5 tactical shooter with destructible environments. High strategy and communication define this title.

5v5 · Bo3 Season 3

Overwatch 2

OW2 · Team Shooter

6v6 hero-based team shooter. Coordinate roles, counter the enemy comp, and dominate objectives.

5v5 · Bo3 Season 3
Getting Started

How It Works

From signup to season play in three steps.

01

Register Your School

Submit your school's info through our contact form. We verify your .edu affiliation and assign your conference.

02

Build Your Roster

Invite players from your campus. Each player signs up with their .edu email and selects their primary title.

03

Compete & Climb

Play scheduled matches, submit results through the portal, and watch your school climb the conference standings.

04

Win Championships

Top schools from each conference advance to the season championship. Prove your campus is the best in the nation.

Ready to compete?

Bring Your Campus to the League

Registration is free. All you need is a .edu email and a team of players ready to compete.
