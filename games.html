Super Smash Bros
Fighting · Platform

The most celebrated fighting game on college campuses. NCEO Smash runs two formats: 1v1 double elimination for individual play, and crew battles where 5-player school teams go head-to-head in back-to-back matches.

Sports · Team

Rocket League is one of the most-watched collegiate esports titles worldwide. Team-based vehicular soccer with high skill expression. Matches run in a best-of-3 series with 3-minute overtime rules.

Fortnite
Battle Royale

NCEO Fortnite is played as a 2v2 Kill Race — the first duo to reach the kill target wins the match. Pure aggression, no placement padding. Fast rounds that reward coordination and gunfighting over survival tactics.
