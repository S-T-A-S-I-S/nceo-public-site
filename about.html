Our Story

About NCEO

Built by collegiate players, for collegiate players. We're creating a legitimate competitive pathway for students across the country.

Our Mission

Building the Future of Collegiate Esports

The National Collegiate Esports Organization (NCEO) exists to give college students a structured, competitive, and community-driven place to compete in the games they love.

We believe competitive gaming builds real skills — teamwork, communication, strategic thinking, and resilience. Our league gives those skills a stage.

From casual players looking for organized competition to serious competitors aiming for the top of their conference, NCEO has a home for everyone with a .edu email and a passion for esports.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Everything we build is grounded in these three pillars.

🏆

Competitive Integrity

Fair, rule-based competition with transparent standings and verified results. Every match matters and every win is earned.

🤝

Community First

Esports is at its best when it brings people together. We invest in the community — not just the tournament bracket.

🎓

Student-Centered

This is built for students, by students. We keep the barrier to entry low and the competitive ceiling high.

📈

Player Development

We want players to grow — in skill, in confidence, and in the broader esports ecosystem. Visibility is part of what we offer.

⚖️

Accessibility

No paywalls, no gatekeeping. If you have a .edu email and a team, you're in. We run on passion, not budgets.

🌐

National Reach

Eight conferences spanning the country — East, West, North, South, Midwest, Pacific, Atlantic, and Central.

Structure

Conference Format

Schools compete within their regional conference before advancing to national championship play.

01

Regular Season

Schools play a round-robin schedule within their conference across all registered titles.

02

Conference Playoffs

Top teams from each conference advance to a single-elimination playoff bracket.

03

Championship

Conference winners compete in the national championship for the NCEO season title.
